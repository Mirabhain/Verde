From your farm to a clear plan

Four steps, no spreadsheets, and you stay in charge of the decision.

  1. Add your farm

    Drop a pin on the map, then enter land size, soil, fertiliser and what you grew.

  2. We read NASA data

    Rainfall, temperature, humidity and soil moisture for your exact coordinates, fetched live.

  3. Compare rotations

    See your current rotation and up to three alternatives, each with reasons, benefits and risks.

  4. Plan the season

    Use the calendar and the wet and dry month guide, and ask the assistant when something is unclear.

What you get

Everything uses real NASA observations and your own farm details.

🛰️

NASA analysis for your pin

Rain, temperature, humidity, evaporation, solar radiation and soil wetness, with charts for the last 120 days and a ten-year typical year.

🔄

Rotation comparison

Your current rotation against three alternatives, each scored with the rules shown on the card.

💧

Soil and water outlook

Soil moisture from NASA plus a nutrient outlook from your crops and fertiliser. Honest about what needs a lab test.

📅

Wet and dry months

See when moisture-related disease or drought stress is more likely at your farm.

💬

AI assistant

Ask about pests, soil or your results. It knows your farm profile and NASA numbers.

ℹ️

A guide, not a replacement for your judgment

Satellite data describes conditions for the area, not your exact plot, and scores are rule-based estimates. Confirm important planting decisions with Jabatan Pertanian Malaysia or MARDI.
